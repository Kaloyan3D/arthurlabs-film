# Phase 4 delivery

The three Remotion compositions are `SentryScene` (1,020 frames), `ArthurLabsFinale` (300 frames) and `SentryFinaleSequence` (1,320 frames). Their exports are `renders/previews/sentry-v1.mp4`, `renders/previews/arthurlabs-finale-v1.mp4` and `renders/previews/sentry-finale-v1.mp4`.

The visual revision tightened the code focus, added moving signals to the review routes and replaced the flat convergence with faceted geometry carried into the final seam. The final master is assembled with `sh scripts/render/stitch-hackathon-master.sh`. That script uses the locked 136-second cut directly. The pre-existing `ArthurLabsFilm` composition remains a setup slate.
