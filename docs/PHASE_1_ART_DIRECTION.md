# A single thought

The opening takes place inside a violet-black optical chamber. A faceted seed catches a cold rim light. It gives rise to three interlocking branches, each with its own center of gravity. Fine filaments carry a single impulse into each branch. The camera makes a continuous lateral arc, then closes in as the branches drift apart. One connection takes on an amber tint and visibly breaks.

The final movement brings the branches back to the seed. A horizontal trace holds for a beat as the light falls away and the ARTHURLABS title resolves in widely spaced capitals. The title is typography set for this film. The network is an abstract metaphor for the narration.

The score follows the same motion. A low two-note bed introduces the chamber, resonant ticks mark the new connections, and detuned tension gives way to an open chord under the title.
